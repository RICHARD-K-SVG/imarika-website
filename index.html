Who we are

A strong beginning, built together

Imarika Pre-Primary School is a learning community in Iyumbu, Dodoma, serving children from Playgroup through Standard 4. We bring early learning and primary education together in a supportive environment where every child can build confidence and a love of learning.

  • Learning through curiosity, practice and play
  • Care, respect and encouragement for every learner
  • Strong foundations in academic and social skills
