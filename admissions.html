Admissions

Joining the Imarika Pre-Primary School family in four simple steps.

Inquire

Parents reach out to express interest and ask initial questions about the school.

Visit & Tour

Parents are invited to visit the school and take a tour of the facilities.

Submit Application

Parents complete and submit the admission application form.

Confirmation

Parents receive a confirmation of their application and next steps.

Requirements Checklist

  • Birth certificate copy
  • Passport photos

Note: Make sure you follow the rules IMARIKA Pre-Primary School.

Ready to Apply?

Get in touch and our admissions team will guide you through the process.
